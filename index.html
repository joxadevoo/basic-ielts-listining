How to use this app

1
Choose a track

Open the Tracks tab and click any audio. The PDF will stay where you scroll it.

2
Control listening

Use play, previous, next, 5-second rewind, speed, volume, and A-B repeat from the bottom player.

3
Use the book freely

Scroll the PDF by yourself. Unit and answer buttons above the PDF are optional shortcuts.

4
Practice actively

Write what you hear in Dictation, save vocabulary in Notebook, and your progress will be remembered.
